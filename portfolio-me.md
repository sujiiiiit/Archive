- **Spring physics:** the zoom amount ramps on a **mass-spring-damper** curve, and in auto mode the focus **spring-follows the cursor** with a lag leash and an edge-snap dead zone. It's pure and deterministic, so preview and export are byte-identical.
- Mapping cursor data from source time and display space onto the timeline and artboard, with trimmed gaps dropped.
- Tests: auto-zoom generation, zoom spring, cursor track, auto-zoom scene.

*Cursor:* "Stabilize cursor type" debounces brief cursor-shape flicker, the click press scale is tuned to 0.78, and predictive intent smooths the follow.
